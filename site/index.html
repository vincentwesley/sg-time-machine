About & download

SG Time Machine saves Singapore's live open data on a schedule and lets you scrub back through it. Nothing here is a forecast or an official record. It is a log of what the public feeds said when we asked.

Limits

  • The archive starts on its first run. There is no earlier history.
  • Scheduled runs can be late or skipped, so gaps happen. A feed with nothing for a day says so.
  • Air-quality readings are drawn at the centre of each NEA region, not at a sensor.
  • Flood alerts can start and end between samples, and the alert radius is archived only from 15 Nov 2026 onward. Where it exists the alert is drawn at its real radius; earlier alerts are a fixed-size marker.
  • Carpark and taxi data come from endpoints whose long-term status is undocumented.

Data sources and licence

Contains information from the following datasets, made available under the terms of the Singapore Open Data Licence version 1.0:

  • PSI and PM2.5 (Real-time API), National Environment Agency.
  • Flood Alerts across Singapore, PUB, Singapore's National Water Agency.
  • Carpark Availability and HDB Carpark Information, Housing & Development Board.
  • Taxi Availability, Land Transport Authority.
  • Dengue Clusters GEOJSON, National Environment Agency.

Basemap: OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles data from © OpenStreetMap contributors.
